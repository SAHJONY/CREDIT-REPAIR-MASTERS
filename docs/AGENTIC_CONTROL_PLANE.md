# CREDIT REPAIR MASTERS — Agentic Control Plane

## Objective

Provide bounded, auditable AI autonomy for consumer and business credit workflows while keeping compliance and external execution authority outside the model.

## Control plane

1. Credit Digital Twin normalizes available credit data.
2. Security Guard scans model-bound text for prompt-injection attempts and minimizes obvious PII.
3. Agent Router selects a primary specialist and supporting agents based on intent.
4. Workflow Planner creates explicit security, analysis, evidence, policy, approval, blocked, or ready states.
5. Compliance Guardian independently evaluates every sensitive proposed action.
6. Agent Safety Evals continuously verify non-bypassable controls.
7. Model Budget Guard caps rounds, tool calls, prompt size, and requested output size.
8. External executors remain disabled until production identity, consent, data-provider, persistence, and compliance gates are verified.

## Non-bypassable rules

The agentic system cannot independently submit disputes, transfer funds, open credit, create identity-theft allegations, fabricate evidence, or bypass consent. High-impact actions are blocked or approval-gated by deterministic local policy.

## Production progression

v0.6 establishes the control plane. The next production layers are durable workflow execution, encrypted persistent storage, authentication/MFA, tenant isolation enforcement, authorized credit-data ingestion, and operational observability.
